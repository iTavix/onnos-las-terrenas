The newest beachfront hotspot in the Dominican Republic.

Located right on the beach in the heart of the village, Onno's Las Terrenas is the place to unwind, eat, drink and dance. Tapas, Tex-Mex and Asian fusion with craft cocktails — feet in the sand.
